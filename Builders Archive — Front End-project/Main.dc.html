SHEET A-006 · ABOUT

About

Builders Archive is a 3D modeling program that turns what you want to build into a step-by-step instruction set, like a model kit. Every step comes with a 3D model of exactly what to add next and the parts you need for it.

01 · DESCRIBE Say what you want Type it in chat, add a sketch or measurements.
02 · MODEL Get the 3D model The whole build, broken into parts with real sizes.
03 · BUILD Follow the steps One step at a time, new parts highlighted, turn it any way you need.
